## Why Sworn and Certified Legal Translations Matter

When dealing with identiteitskaart translations and similar legal documents, sworn translations ensure that the translated text is recognized and holds legal value. Certified legal translators are authorized by judicial authorities to provide translations that are accepted in court proceedings, government applications, and notarial transactions across multilingual jurisdictions.

Key benefits of sworn and certified legal translations:

- **Legal Validity:** Sworn translations are legally binding and accepted by governmental and judicial bodies.
- **Accuracy:** Every detail, from names to legal terminology, is carefully rendered to avoid misinterpretations in international contracts.
- **Trust & Reliability:** Clients can rely on certified legal translators for translations without legal risks.

- **Confidentiality breaches:** Sensitive personal data on identiteitskaarten must be protected at every stage of the translation process.
