## Why Precision Matters in Legal Translations

Legal documents are unique. They are binding, highly specific, and often scrutinized in courts or compliance reviews. An inaccurate legal translation can:

- Cause misinterpretations in international contracts
- Jeopardize compliance with Belgian and international regulations
- Lead to legal risks, disputes, or contract termination
- Delay legalizations & apostilles or immigration processes

Sworn translations and certified legal translators are not just preferred—they are often required by courts, government agencies, and notaries. These translators guarantee that the translated content is a true and faithful representation of the original, recognized as valid and official.

## The Role of Sworn Translators and Certification

Sworn translators in Belgium are officially authorized to provide translations that are legally recognized. Their sworn translations are required for:

- Notarial deeds
- Court documents
- Birth, marriage, and death certificates
- Legalizations & apostilles

Certified legal translators ensure that every legal term, nuance, and clause is conveyed accurately, eliminating the risk of misinterpretation or ambiguity. This level of precision is essential for contract managers, compliance officers, and legal professionals managing multilingual communication across borders.

## Risks of Mistranslations in Legal Contexts

The consequences of a mistranslation in a legal document can be severe:

- **Invalid contracts:** A mistranslated clause can alter the rights and obligations of parties, leading to disputes or unenforceable agreements.
- **Legal penalties:** Non-compliance due to translation errors can result in fines or sanctions.
- **Confidentiality breaches:** Mishandling sensitive information during translation can expose clients to privacy violations.

This is why error correction, thorough proofreading and review, and a robust translation strategy are essential parts of any legal translation process.
