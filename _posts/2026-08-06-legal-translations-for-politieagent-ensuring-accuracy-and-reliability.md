## Why Accuracy Is Non-Negotiable in Legal Translations in Belgium

For cases involving a politieagent, even a minor misinterpretation or ambiguous phrase can lead to significant legal consequences. Whether the document is an official police report, a notarial deed, or an international contract, every word must precisely capture the original intent. Sworn translations and certified legal translators provide the assurance that legal documents will hold up in court and in front of compliance officers across Belgium.

### Risks of Mistranslations in Legal Contexts

- **Legal liabilities:** Errors may invalidate contracts or compromise investigations.
- **Delayed processes:** Incorrect translations can stall legalizations, apostilles, or compliance checks.
- **International confusion:** Misinterpretations in international contracts can trigger cross-border legal disputes.
- **Loss of trust:** Inaccurate documents undermine the reliability of law enforcement and judicial proceedings.

## The Role of Sworn and Certified Translators in Belgium

Sworn translators, recognized by Belgian authorities, ensure that translations of official documents—such as those involving a politieagent—are accurate, certified, and legally valid. Certified legal translators go beyond language skills, bringing deep knowledge of legal terminology, context, and requirements. Their sworn translations are accepted by courts, notaries, and public institutions across Belgium and internationally.
