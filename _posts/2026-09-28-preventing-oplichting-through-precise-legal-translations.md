Oplichting—or fraud—remains a serious concern for legal professionals, corporate legal departments, and public institutions across Belgium. In legal proceedings, contracts, and official documents, even a minor mistranslation can open the door to costly misunderstandings, compliance failures, or, in the worst cases, intentional deception. That is why accuracy, compliance, and confidentiality are not just expectations, but necessities in legal translations.

## Why Accuracy in Legal Translations Is Non-Negotiable

Legal translations are far more than direct word-for-word conversions. Each contract clause, notarial deed, or official document must be interpreted with unwavering precision. Misinterpretations in international contracts, for example, can lead to oplichting by providing loopholes or ambiguous terms that can be exploited.

- **International contracts**: A single mistranslated term can alter obligations, creating opportunities for fraud or disputes.
- **Notarial deeds**: Inaccuracies can render a legal act invalid, exposing parties to unnecessary legal risks.
- **Official documents**: When used for immigration, compliance, or court proceedings, errors can result in costly delays or even legal penalties.

## The Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators

Ensuring that translations hold up in court or in compliance checks requires more than linguistic skill. Sworn translations by certified legal translators provide an extra layer of reliability. These professionals are authorized by the courts in Belgium and meet stringent standards for accuracy and impartiality.

- **Sworn translations**: For documents that need legal validity, such as apostilles and legalizations, only sworn translators are recognized.
- **Certified legal translators**: Their expertise guarantees not only linguistic accuracy but also compliance with Belgian and international legal standards.

## Risks of Mistranslations in Legal Contexts

Fraudsters often look for vulnerabilities in legal documents. An imprecise translation can lead to:

- **Oplichting through ambiguity**: Ambiguous or incorrect terms can be exploited in court or during contract execution.
- **Error correction costs**: Rectifying mistakes after the fact can be expensive, time-consuming, and may not always fully resolve legal risks.
- **Loss of trust**: Legal professionals and clients depend on flawless multilingual communication. Errors can damage reputations and client relationships.

## Choosing a Reliable Legal Translation Agency

The stakes are too high to leave legal translation to chance. When selecting a translation agency, prioritize:

- **Quality over speed**: Rushed jobs increase the risk of misinterpretations and errors.
- **Tailor-made solutions and personal approach**: Every legal case is unique, and translations should be adapted accordingly.
- **Trust and reliability**: Look for agencies with proven track records in handling sensitive documents confidentially.
- **Translations without legal risks**: Ensure the agency provides comprehensive proofreading and review as part of their translation strategy.
