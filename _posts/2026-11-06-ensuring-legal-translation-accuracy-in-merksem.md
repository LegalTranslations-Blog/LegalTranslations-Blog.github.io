## Why Legal Translations Matter in Merksem

### Critical Elements in Legal Document Translation

- **Accuracy:** Legal terminology is complex and context-dependent. A single mistranslation can alter the intent of a clause or introduce ambiguities, leading to misinterpretations in international contracts or official documents.
- **Compliance:** Belgian regulations often require certified translations or sworn translators for legalizations, apostilles, and notarial deeds. Non-compliance can compromise the validity of documents presented in court or to public authorities.
- **Confidentiality:** Legal documents frequently contain sensitive information. Trust and reliability are essential to safeguard client privacy and uphold professional standards.

## The Role of Sworn and Certified Translators

A sworn translation, performed by a certified legal translator, carries official status in Belgium. These translations are recognized by courts, public institutions, and government agencies. Sworn translators guarantee that every word accurately reflects the original, supporting error correction and reducing legal risks.

### Risks of Mistranslations

- **Legal Risks:** Misinterpretations in international contracts can lead to disputes or unenforceable agreements.
- **Compliance Failures:** Inaccurate translations may not meet requirements for legalizations or apostilles.
- **Financial Impact:** Errors can result in costly litigation or lost business opportunities.
