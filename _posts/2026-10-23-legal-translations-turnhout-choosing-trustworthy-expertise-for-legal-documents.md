## The Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators in Belgium

In Belgium, many legal documents require sworn translations. Sworn translators are authorized by the courts to provide certified translations that are recognized by public institutions and government agencies. Certified legal translators understand the nuances of legal terminology in both source and target languages, ensuring:

- Flawless accuracy in international contracts  
- Reliable translations for legalizations and apostilles  
- Adherence to regional legal standards
