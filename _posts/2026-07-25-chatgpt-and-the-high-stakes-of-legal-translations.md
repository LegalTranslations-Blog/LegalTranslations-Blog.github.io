Legal professionals in Belgium increasingly encounter AI-powered tools like ChatGPT promising quick translations. While these technologies offer convenience, legal translations require a level of precision, compliance, and confidentiality that only specialist agencies can guarantee. In matters of international contracts, notarial deeds, and official documents, a single misinterpretation can have costly consequences.

## Why Accuracy, Compliance, and Confidentiality Are Non-Negotiable in Legal Translations

Legal documents are binding texts—contracts, court decisions, company statutes, or immigration papers—that define rights, obligations, and liabilities. Even minor errors in legal translations can lead to:

- **Ambiguities in international contracts**
- **Invalid notarial deeds**
- **Compliance breaches**
- **Disputes or litigation**

Moreover, legal translation services demand strict confidentiality. Sensitive information must remain protected, which is not always guaranteed when using generic AI platforms or unvetted providers.

## The Critical Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators in Belgium

Legal translations often require sworn translations—officially certified by a sworn translator who is recognized by Belgian courts or notaries. These certified legal translators:

- Possess in-depth legal knowledge and language expertise
- Provide certified legal translators’ stamps and signatures
- Ensure translations are accepted by courts, government agencies, and public institutions

Certified legal translators not only translate, but also safeguard the integrity and legal standing of translated documents.

## Risks of Misinterpretations in International Contracts and Legal Documents

Misinterpretations in international contracts or official translations can result in:

- Unenforceable agreements
- Regulatory fines
- Lost business opportunities
- Damaged reputations

A reliable translation agency puts quality over speed, focusing on error correction, thorough proofreading and review, and tailor-made solutions for each client’s needs. This ensures translations without legal risks.
