## Why Accuracy Matters in Legal Translations

Legal documents are unforgiving of error. A single mistranslation in an international contract or notarial deed can result in costly disputes, regulatory penalties, or even the invalidation of an agreement. For organizations operating through gateways such as **Aéroport de Leipzig-Halle**, where legal and logistical lines intersect, the margin for error narrows even further.

### The Risks of Misinterpretations in International Contracts

- **Ambiguities:** Misinterpretations in international contracts can lead to confusion about parties’ obligations or rights.
- **Legal Risks:** Inaccurate translations may render a contract unenforceable or expose parties to legal action.
- **Compliance Issues:** Failure to meet local and international legal standards can halt operations and damage reputation.

Certified legal translators add an extra layer of assurance, confirming that the translation is both complete and accurate.
