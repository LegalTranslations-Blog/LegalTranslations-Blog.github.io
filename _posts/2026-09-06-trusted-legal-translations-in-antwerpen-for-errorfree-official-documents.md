## Why Accuracy Matters in Legal Document Translation in Belgium

Legal documents—including international contracts, notarial deeds, and official records—require precision. Each term, clause, and statement must be rendered with exact meaning in the target language. The consequences of even minor errors can include:

- **Misinterpretations in international contracts** leading to litigation or financial loss  
- **Invalid notarial deeds** that are rejected by courts or public agencies  
- **Compliance failures** with Belgian and EU regulations  
- **Legal risks** from inaccurate terminology or missing information  

A professional translation agency specialising in legal translations ensures that every word holds up under scrutiny—whether in a courtroom, government office, or boardroom in Antwerpen and across Belgium.

## The Essential Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators in Antwerpen

In Antwerpen, and across Belgium, many legal documents require sworn translations or certified legal translators. These professionals are authorised to provide translations that are officially recognised by courts, notaries, and public authorities.

A sworn translation is more than a linguistic service—it is a legal act. Certified legal translators in Belgium stamp and sign their work, attesting to its completeness and accuracy. This is vital for:

- **International contracts** that must meet cross-border legal standards  
- **Legalizations and apostilles** for use in foreign jurisdictions  
- **Documents submitted to government agencies and courts**
