## The Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators in Belgium

Sworn translations, often required for official use, are performed by certified legal translators who have been authorized by the Belgian authorities. These professionals guarantee that translations of notarial deeds, court documents, and immigration paperwork are legally valid and recognized.

Certified legal translators adhere to strict ethical standards, ensuring:

- Absolute confidentiality for sensitive content  
- Complete accuracy, eliminating misinterpretations in international contracts  
- Legal compliance with Belgian and international requirements  
- Reliable error correction and thorough proofreading and review processes  

## The Risks of Mistranslations in Legal Contexts and International Contracts

In legal matters, even minor translation errors can have major repercussions. Misinterpretations in international contracts might lead to:

- Invalid agreements  
- Regulatory penalties  
- Legal actions and lengthy disputes  
- Damaged cross-border relationships  

For example, an incorrect translation of a compliance clause or an ambiguous term in a contract can expose a business or public institution to unnecessary legal risks.
