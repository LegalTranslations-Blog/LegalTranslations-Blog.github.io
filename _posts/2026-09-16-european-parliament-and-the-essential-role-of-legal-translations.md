## Why Accuracy Matters in Legal Translations in Belgium

Legal documents such as international contracts, notarial deeds, and official documents are binding. A single misinterpretation or ambiguous translation can result in significant legal risks, from contractual disputes to non-compliance with European standards. In the context of the European Parliament, where multilingual communication is a daily reality, the need for high-accuracy legal translations is even more pronounced.

**Key risks of poor translations in legal contexts include:**

- **Misinterpretations in international contracts** leading to costly litigation  
- **Compliance failures** with EU directives and regulations  
- **Loss of trust and credibility** with partners and authorities  
- **Unenforceable agreements** due to translation errors

## The Crucial Role of Sworn and Certified Translators in Legal Translation Services

The European Parliament relies on sworn translations and certified legal translators to ensure that every word maintains its legal value across languages. Sworn translators are authorized to provide legally recognized translations, stamped and signed for official use in courts, notaries, and government agencies in Belgium. This is essential for:

- **Legalizations and apostilles** required for cross-border documents  
- **Proofreading and review** to eliminate even the smallest errors  
- **Translations without legal risks** through rigorous error correction and quality assurance

Certified legal translators bring not only linguistic expertise but also a deep understanding of legal terminology and procedures. Their work upholds confidentiality, which is paramount in sensitive legal matters.
