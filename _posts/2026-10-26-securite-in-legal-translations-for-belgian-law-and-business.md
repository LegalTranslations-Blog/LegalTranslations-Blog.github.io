For law firms, corporations, and public institutions in Belgium, these principles are not optional—they are essential safeguards. A single misinterpretation in an international contract or a notarial deed can have far-reaching legal and financial consequences. ## Why Accuracy Matters in Legal Translations

Legal documents are precise by design. Every word, clause, and nuance carries potential legal weight. Inaccurate translations can introduce ambiguity, leading to:

- **Misinterpretations in international contracts**
- **Invalid notarial deeds**
- **Non-compliance with regulatory standards**
- **Costly disputes or legal risks**

## The Role of Sworn and Certified Legal Translators

In Belgium, many official documents require sworn translations, executed by translators who are officially recognised by the courts. These professionals are not only linguists but also experts in legal terminology, procedure, and confidentiality. Certified legal translators add an extra layer of credibility, especially for documents involved in legalisations & apostilles or those submitted to government agencies.

Key benefits of working with sworn and certified translators:

- **Legally valid translations for court and administrative use**
- **Expertise in multilingual communication for complex, cross-border matters**
- **Guaranteed confidentiality and trustworthiness**
- **Minimised risk of misinterpretation or incomplete translation**

## The Risks of Mistranslation in Legal Contexts

A minor translation error has the potential to escalate into a critical legal issue. For example, an incorrectly translated clause in an international contract may alter obligations, expose parties to liability, or invalidate the agreement entirely. The same applies to translations of official documents like birth certificates, immigration papers, or corporate statutes. These risks highlight the importance of engaging a translation agency that prioritises trust, reliability, and security.
